Image sizes

Upload sizes for desktop and mobile images. Most sections are in the Theme editor (Online Store › Themes › Customize). These images crop to fill their space, so keep the subject and any text in the centre.

Hero banner (Builder Hero Image)

Theme editor › Slideshow Hero section › Builder Hero Image block.

  • Tablets show the desktop image, cropped narrower. Keep the subject in the middle 70% of the width.
  • With Fixed height Layout turned on, the banner is a short strip. Upload 2560 × 1080px and keep the subject in the middle band.

Video banner (v2 Banner Video)

Theme editor › v2 Banner Video section. Add a video and an image for each screen size. The image shows while the video loads, or if it can't play.

  • On short screens the top and bottom are cut off. Keep text and subjects in the vertical middle.
