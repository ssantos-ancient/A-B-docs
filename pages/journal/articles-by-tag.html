Articles by tag

Tags decide where Journal articles appear. Add tags to a post in Shopify admin › Content › Blog posts › [post] › Tags. Article lists are set up in the Theme editor (Online Store › Themes › Customize).

Show articles with a tag on a page

  1. In the Theme editor, open the page and click Add section › Content Blog.
  2. In Blog, choose Journal.
  3. Type the tag in Narrow by tag, spelled exactly as it is on the articles.
  4. Add a title and click Save.

If you leave the tag empty, it shows the latest 10 articles.

Show related posts under an article

  1. Give the article a tag that is marked Featured (see below).
  2. Up to 12 articles with the same tag now show under it automatically.

Name a tag for shoppers

Set the label shoppers see for a tag, and choose which tags are featured.

  1. Go to Shopify admin › Content › Metaobjects › Tag translations and click Add entry.
  2. Tag title: the tag exactly as it is on the articles, including capitals.
  3. Tag translation: the label shoppers see.
  4. Turn on Featured to show the tag in the Journal tag bar and use it for related posts.
  5. Optional: add a description and icon. Click Save.

Show an article in the menu

  1. Tag the article nav:bestsellers or nav:science.
  2. It appears in the menu's Articles block (up to 5 articles). See Navigation.

Use special tags

TagWhat it does
hideHides the article from article lists. It still shows in the menu's Articles block.
nav:…Shows the article in the header menu.
market_xxShows the article in that market, for example market_de for Germany.

A market with Enable market articles turned on (in Shopify admin › Settings › Markets › [market] › Metafields) shows only articles tagged for that market.
