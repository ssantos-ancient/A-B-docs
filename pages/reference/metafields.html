Metafields & Metaobjects

Every extra field the Ancient + Brave website uses, what it does and where it shows on the site. Click the guide link for step-by-step help.

What they are

  • Metafield: an extra field on a product, article, collection, page or market. Open the item in Shopify admin and scroll to Metafields at the bottom.
  • Metaobject: a reusable entry, such as an author or a badge. Create it once in Shopify admin › Content › Metaobjects, then select it in a metafield.

Names in Shopify admin may differ slightly from the names here. Short forms you'll see: ATC (add to cart), OTP (one-time purchase), PDP (product page), PLP (product listing page, or collection page). Buy box means the add to cart area on the product page.
