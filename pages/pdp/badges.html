Badges & Labels

Add badges, ribbons, icons and short messages to a product. You set them all on the product in Shopify admin › Products › [product] › Metafields.

Add a badge

A round badge in the corner of the first product image on the product page.

  1. Go to Shopify admin › Content › Metaobjects › Product badge and create an entry.
  2. Paste the badge artwork code into SVG. Ask the design team for it.
  3. Set Position to left or right, exactly as written here.
  4. Go to Shopify admin › Products › [product] › Metafields › Product badge, add the entry and click Save.
  • You can add up to 6 badges per side. If there are several on one side, they take turns every 3 seconds.
  • Any other position, even with an extra space, hides the badge.

Add a ribbon

Fill in one of these in Shopify admin › Products › [product] › Metafields:

  • Promo tag: a text ribbon at the top right, for example "New". It's hidden when the product has a badge.
  • Promo tag left: a text ribbon at the top left. It always shows.

Add an icon to the product image

  1. Go to Shopify admin › Content › Metaobjects › Product image icon and create an entry with up to 2 icons and their alt text (a short description of each icon).
  2. Go to Shopify admin › Products › [product] › Metafields › Product image icon and select the entry.
  3. In Product image icon position, choose Top left (default), Top right, Bottom left or Bottom right, then click Save.

The icon sits on top of badges and ribbons, so put it in a different corner.

Add a message under the review stars

Fill in Shopify admin › Products › [product] › Metafields › PDP below review stars message. It shows as a pill under the stars on the product page (PDP), for example "Our bestseller".

Add a label to product cards

Product cards are the small product tiles in collections and sliders. Fill in one of these in Shopify admin › Products › [product] › Metafields:

  • Product card top left pill: a pill on the product card.
  • Left / Right content product card: corner text on product cards in a grid.
