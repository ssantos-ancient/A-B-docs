Promos & Products

Turn product page sections on and off, and set up free shipping and gifts. Most of this is in the Theme editor (Online Store › Themes › Customize). For badges, subscriptions and sibling products, see Badges & Labels, Subscriptions and Sibling products.

Turn product page sections on or off

Go to Theme editor, open any product page (select it from the dropdown at the top) and click the Product pages v2 section. Changes apply to every product that uses the same template.

Set up the free shipping bar

  1. Go to Theme editor › Theme settings › Carts and turn on Enable.
  2. Set the Shipping threshold in pence, for example 4000 for £40.
  3. Write the progress message and the success message, then click Save.

Set up a gift with purchase

  1. Go to Theme editor › Theme settings › In cart threshold offer and turn on Enable in cart offer.
  2. Set the Free gift threshold in pence.
  3. Choose the Gift product and up to two variants.
  4. Add the teaser image and text. To show the teaser before the shopper reaches the threshold, turn on Show teaser.
  5. Click Save.

Add a cart add-on or subscriber gift

Set these in Shopify admin › Products › [product] › Metafields:

  • Cross sell product: offers another product as an Add on + in the cart. Write its text in Cross sell description on the add-on product.
  • Auto add product: a free gift for shoppers who subscribe.
